sources.json records the immutable upstream revisions, Git blob SHA-1 values and original sample paths. Files are converted to mono 44.1 kHz MP3, limited to 8 seconds, with a short fade at the end. No songs or third-party compositions are included. Original SFZ pitch centers are retained. Guitar/bass samples use two velocity layers, drums use three. Unsampled notes use the nearest recorded root and pitch shifting.
